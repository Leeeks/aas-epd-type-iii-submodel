# Normative References

This Submodel Template is based on the following core standards:

1. **ISO 14025**: Environmental labels and declarations — Type III environmental declarations — Principles and procedures.
2. **EN 15804**: Sustainability of construction works - Environmental product declarations - Core rules for the product category of construction products.
3. **EN 50693**: Product category rules for life cycle assessments of electronic and electrical products and systems.
4. **PEP Ecopassport PCR**: Product Category Rules for Electrical, Electronic and HVAC-R Products.
5. **AAS Part 1**: Details of the Asset Administration Shell - Part 1: Metamodel Version 3.0.
